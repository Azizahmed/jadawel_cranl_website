# Documents, brochures, reports, and PDF

Apply these rules to brochures, one-pagers, proposals, reports, manuals, letter-size/A4 documents, and print-ready PDF exports.

## Composition

- Put the main subject, outcome, or title before brand decoration.
- Use the complete logo on the cover and, when needed, a smaller signature in the back page or footer.
- Build pages on a clear grid with generous margins. For Arabic, anchor headings and reading columns to the right.
- Use blue for section openings, calls to action, or key figures; keep long reading surfaces white or Cloud.
- Use the vertical bar and diamond as occasional navigation devices, not a repeating wallpaper.

## Content hierarchy

- Cover: direct title, concise descriptor, logo, and only essential metadata.
- Interior: one primary heading level, one secondary level, readable body copy, and consistent captions.
- Data: label units and sources, highlight only the most important value in blue, and keep status colors semantic.
- Bilingual documents: keep full Arabic and English passages in separate directional blocks or sections when mixing would destabilize reading order.

## Print production

- Use the CMYK approximations in the identity system only as a starting point; obtain a printer proof for final production.
- Respect the printer's requested bleed, trim, safety margin, color profile, and image resolution.
- Use images at effective print resolution appropriate to the final size; do not upscale the raster logo beyond its native dimensions.
- Embed or outline legally supplied fonts according to the production workflow. If Thmanyah Sans cannot be embedded, resolve licensing or provide a disclosed editable-source exception before final print.

## PDF completion criteria

Inspect every exported page. Arabic must remain logical and readable, logos must be sharp, links and page order must work, no text may clip or reflow, and the PDF must match the editable source at representative zoom levels. State whether the final print profile and physical proof were verified.
