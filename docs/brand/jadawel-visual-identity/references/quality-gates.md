# Quality gates

Apply the relevant checks before calling a branded artifact complete.

## Brand fidelity

- The supplied master logo file is used without redrawing, stretching, recoloring, or effects.
- The selected composite logo variant has sufficient contrast and one-cell clear space.
- The wordmark and five-square grid symbol remain one locked unit with their original spacing and color relationship.
- The grid symbol is not presented alone as a logo or application icon.
- The diamond remains a supporting device rather than a substitute logo.
- Jadawel Ink, Blue, Cloud, White, and state colors retain their defined roles.
- Thmanyah Sans is used when licensed and available; any fallback is disclosed.

## Language and direction

- Arabic is logical-order Unicode and paragraphs use native RTL behavior.
- English uses LTR behavior and sentence case.
- Mixed numbers, units, URLs, and technical terms display in the intended order.
- The final output is inspected rather than assumed correct from source text.

## Layout and communication

- The hierarchy is clear at the real viewing size.
- Open space, alignment, and the six-column principle support the content.
- Blue identifies focus instead of becoming visual noise.
- Text, diagrams, tables, and screenshots do not clip, overlap, or become illegible.
- Decorative choices do not compete with product truth or the primary message.

## Medium-specific proof

- **Web/product:** inspect real responsive RTL and LTR pages, keyboard focus, contrast, loading, and console errors.
- **PDF/print:** inspect every page, confirm page size and export fidelity, and disclose unverified printer profile or physical proof.
- **Presentation:** render every slide, inspect each slide, verify Arabic direction and fonts, and disclose the target applications actually tested.
- **Images/social:** inspect exact output dimensions, safe areas, legibility at feed size, and logo sharpness.

## Handoff

Deliver the editable source when applicable, the final export, and the exact logo assets used. Name deviations, missing inputs, raster limitations, and unrun checks separately. A valid file is not sufficient when the visible result fails the identity.
